Version 3.1 is here – we've made some important changes

Update 3.1

Thursday, September 17th, 2026 · 12:00 AM

You're now competing for some cash prizes!

  • 1st place – $50
  • 2nd place – $25
  • 3rd place – $25

Also, please note the changes we've made to Sort Circuit:

  • The progressive difficulty of the game has been increased – now, it's harder to break into the 900+ points range. As a result, we've decreased the scores of all top competitors on the leaderboard proportionally.
  • When you enter your student ID to submit your score, we'll automatically grab your name. Your name and school year are the only two things displayed on the leaderboard. If, for whatever reason, your ID is missing from our database, please email ecojustice@brophybroncos.org.
  • Please refrain from entering someone else's student ID into the leaderboard. Don't attempt to cheat or “hack” the game to post an illegitimate score to the leaderboard. It ruins the fun for everyone. Anyone caught doing either is subject to receiving a JUG.
